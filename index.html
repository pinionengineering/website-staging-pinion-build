Pinion Minions

These are IPFS nodes with a little extra magic. Our minions run atop a robust distributed storage layer, providing high availability, replication, and strong durability guarantees.

Minions are why we can guarantee high uptime through network partitions and node failures. Got a 404 from one gateway but found the content on another? Ours don't do that. Lost content after a node went down? Ours don't do that either.
